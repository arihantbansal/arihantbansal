# Arihant Bansal

I'm an engineer working on agents and developer tools. I like starting with the product question and staying through the awkward edge cases.

These days, I'm thinking about tools for long-running agents: how people understand what they're doing, step in when needed, and leave them alone the rest of the time.

While at university, I joined [Elusiv](https://www.elusiv.io/) as its third employee and stayed through its transition to [Arcium](https://arcium.com/). I worked across product and engineering and co-authored the [Arcium Purplepaper](https://www.arcium.com/research/purplepaper).

## Selected work

- [Default Apps for Raycast](https://github.com/arihantbansal/default-apps-raycast) — view and change the default macOS app for each file type from Raycast
- [Solana Keypair Search](https://github.com/arihantbansal/solana-keypair-search) — find forgotten Solana keypairs on your device and see whether they still hold funds
- [UnifyFL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.18916) — cross-silo federated learning without a central aggregator, published at Middleware 2025
